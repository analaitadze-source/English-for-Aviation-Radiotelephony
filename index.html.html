MAYDAY MAYDAY MAYDAY

ICAO DEFINITION & DIALOGUE
ICAO Standard Definition:

Distress signal used to indicate grave and imminent danger requiring immediate assistance. Takes precedence over all other communications.

Standard Pilot-ATC Radio Dialogue:
PILOT: "MAYDAY MAYDAY MAYDAY, Speedbird 452, Engine failure on climb out, turning back to airfield, heading 090."
ATC: "Speedbird 452, Roger MAYDAY, cleared ILS runway 27R, wind 260 at 12 knots, squawk 7700."
