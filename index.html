Radiotelephony examples

These are fragments, not complete transmissions. Use the applicable local phraseology and readback procedures.

request pushback
A pilot asks for approval to move back from the stand.
taxi to holding point
A controller instructs an aircraft to move to a specified holding position.
hold short of runway
Stop before the specified runway and read back the instruction.
cross runway
A controller's runway crossing clearance must be read back.
cleared to land
A landing clearance includes the runway to be read back.
go around / going around
A controller may instruct a go-around; a pilot reports going around.

General aviation English

Useful ordinary English, not prescribed radio transmissions.

file a flight plan
Submit the planned flight details.
conduct a pre-flight inspection
Check the aircraft before departure.
encounter turbulence
Experience irregular air movement during flight.
extend the landing gear
Lower the landing gear.
perform an instrument approach
Fly an approach using instrument guidance.
divert to an alternate aerodrome
Fly to another suitable aerodrome instead.
maintain situational awareness
Keep track of the aircraft and its surroundings.
